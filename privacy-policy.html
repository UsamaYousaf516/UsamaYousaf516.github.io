Privacy Policy

Arrow Escape by Yaro Technology · Last updated 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Arrow Escape (the "App") collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. The App is published by Yaro Technology ("we", "us", "our").

The App does not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

1. Information stored only on your device

Your game progress is saved on your phone and is not sent to us. This includes your current level, stars, coins, hints, daily puzzle calendar, streak, trophies, chosen themes and your settings (sound, music, vibration, accessibility options and reminder time).

Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage in Android settings deletes this information. We cannot restore it.

2. Information collected automatically

To keep the App working well and to understand how it is played, the App uses services from Google that collect some information automatically:

  • Google Firebase Analytics: how you use the App (for example screens viewed, levels started and finished, hints used), app version, device model, operating system version, language, approximate country derived from your IP address, and a random app instance identifier.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports and errors, including the state of the App and device when the problem happened (device model, operating system version, free memory and storage, orientation) and a random installation identifier.

This information is not used to identify you personally. You can turn it off at any time in Settings > Share usage and crash data.

3. Advertising

The App is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob, including optional rewarded videos that give you a hint or an extra heart.

AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, in order to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. If you agree, it may also use this information to show ads that are more relevant to you.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time from the App's settings.

You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your phone).

Learn how Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Purchases

If you buy an in-app product (such as removing ads), the payment is handled by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details. Google tells the App which product was bought so it can be delivered and restored on a new device.

5. Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the App asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; we do not run a server that sends them and no information is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in the App or in Android settings.

6. How we use information

  • To run the App and its features, such as saving progress, daily puzzles and rewards.
  • To find and fix crashes and bugs.
  • To understand which levels are too easy or too hard and improve the game.
  • To show ads that keep the App free, and to deliver purchases.

We do not sell your personal information.

7. Sharing

We share information only with the service providers named above (Google Firebase, Google AdMob and Google Play), who process it on our behalf or as independent controllers under their own privacy policies, or when the law requires us to. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

These providers may process information on servers outside your country, including in the United States, with safeguards required by law.

8. How long information is kept

Information on your device is kept until you uninstall the App or clear its data. Analytics data is kept by Google for no longer than 14 months, and crash reports for 90 days. Advertising data is kept by Google under its own retention policies.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Turn off analytics and crash reporting in Settings > Share usage and crash data.
  • Change your ads consent in the App's settings, and reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Turn notifications on or off at any time.
  • Delete everything stored on your device by uninstalling the App.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, and to object to or restrict its use. Because the App does not know who you are, please include your app version and the approximate date you used the App when you contact us, so we can help. Email support@yaro.live and we will reply within 30 days.

10. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact support@yaro.live and we will delete it.

11. Security

We use the security measures provided by Google's services, and information sent from the App to them is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect the information we handle.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes or the law requires it. The new version will be posted in the App and on our website with a new "Last updated" date. Please check it from time to time.
