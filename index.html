How to play

No timers and no rush. Every board can be solved; the trick is finding the order.

1. Tap an arrow

It slides straight out of the board in the direction it points.

2. Keep the path clear

If another arrow is in the way, the tap is blocked and costs one of your 3 hearts.

3. Clear the board

Release every arrow to finish the level and earn up to 3 stars.

Questions

Is Arrow Escape free?

Yes. The game is free and shows ads. Optional videos can give you an extra heart or a hint, and you can always earn hints and hearts with coins instead.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Every level and the daily puzzles play offline. Ads need a connection, so rewarded videos are only available when you are online.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up. Your progress is saved on your phone.

What data does the app collect?

Your progress stays on your device. Anonymous usage and crash data help us improve the game, and you can turn that off in Settings. Ads are served by Google AdMob. The full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Which devices are supported?

Android phones running Android 7.0 or newer. An iPhone version is planned.

How do I contact you?

Email support@yaro.live. We read every message.
